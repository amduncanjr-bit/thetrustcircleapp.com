Trust Circle

Walk to class, head home from the library, or go out with friends — knowing your circle is always watching your back.

Campus Life Shouldn't Feel Unsafe

College campuses should be places of growth and discovery, not anxiety. But late-night walks from the library, unfamiliar neighborhoods around campus, and social situations with strangers are real concerns. Trust Circle gives students a personal safety network — connecting them with friends, roommates, and family who can monitor their location and respond instantly if something goes wrong.

Share Location with Your Inner Circle

Set up a trusted circle with your roommates, close friends, and parents. When you're walking home late or heading to an unfamiliar part of town, share your real-time location with your circle. They can see exactly where you are and track your route. If you stop moving unexpectedly or deviate from your path, they'll know something might be wrong.

Campus Emergency Contacts in One Tap

Trust Circle includes quick access to campus police, local emergency services, and campus-specific safety contacts. No more searching your university's website for the right number in a panic. Save your campus police number, RA contact, student health services, and counseling center — all accessible with one tap from the app's main screen.

Consent-Based, Student-Controlled

Trust Circle is built on consent. You decide when to share your location, with whom, and for how long. Parents only see what students choose to share — this isn't a tracking app, it's a safety network that the student controls. Turn sharing on when walking home, turn it off when you're safe. Your privacy, your rules.

Frequently asked

Can my parents track me without my permission?

No. Trust Circle is consent-based. Students must explicitly choose to share their location. Parents only see what students choose to share, and sharing can be stopped at any time.

Is Trust Circle different from Find My Friends?

Yes. Trust Circle goes beyond location sharing with live video streaming, SOS alerts, multi-channel notifications (push/SMS/email), and encrypted document storage. It's a complete safety network, not just a location dot.

Can I add my roommates to my circle?

Absolutely. Add roommates, close friends, study partners, and family. You choose who's in your circle and what they can see.

Does it work inside buildings?

GPS accuracy can vary indoors. Trust Circle uses the best available location data, including Wi-Fi positioning, to provide the most accurate location possible.

Is there a group circle for our friend group?

Yes. You can create a circle with your friend group so everyone can see each other's location when going out, walking home, or traveling together.

How do I add campus police contacts?

In the app, you can save campus police and other emergency numbers for one-tap access. Trust Circle also provides directory features for common campus safety resources.
