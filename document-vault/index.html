Your Most Important Documents, Always Ready

Trust Circle's Document Vault lets you securely store your driver's license, insurance cards, vehicle registration, and other critical documents in an encrypted digital wallet. When you need them — during a traffic stop, an emergency, or at a checkpoint — they're ready in one tap. No fumbling through your wallet, no digging in the glove box.

Encrypted & Private

Your documents are encrypted end-to-end and stored securely. Only you can access them. Trust Circle doesn't read, scan, or share your documents with anyone. When you display them during a traffic stop using Drive With Confidence, the officer can see the screen — but the data never leaves your device or your encrypted cloud storage.

Beyond the Basics

The Document Vault isn't limited to driving documents. Store your passport, medical records, emergency contacts list, or any document you might need in a critical moment. Families can store children's medical information for babysitters, travelers can keep passport copies for emergencies, and students can store campus IDs and insurance information.

Integrated with Trust Circle's Safety Features

Your Document Vault works seamlessly with Drive With Confidence for traffic stop protection, and your trusted circle can be granted access to specific emergency documents if you choose. This means if you're incapacitated, your designated contacts can access critical medical information to help first responders.

Frequently asked

What documents can I store?

You can store any document: driver's license, insurance cards, registration, passport, medical records, emergency contacts, and more. The vault accepts photos and PDFs.

Are my documents safe?

Yes. All documents are encrypted end-to-end. Only you can access them — Trust Circle employees cannot view your stored documents.

Can my trusted circle see my documents?

Only if you explicitly grant them access to specific documents. By default, your documents are private and visible only to you.

What if I lose my phone?

Your documents are encrypted in the cloud and tied to your account. Log in on a new device to regain access. You can also remotely wipe your vault from another device.

Is there a storage limit?

Trust Circle provides generous storage for personal documents. For most users, the free tier is more than sufficient for all essential documents.

Can I use the vault offline?

Yes. Documents stored in the vault are available offline after they've been synced. You can display your license during a traffic stop even without cell service.
