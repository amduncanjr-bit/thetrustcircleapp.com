Be Prepared, Not Scared

The fear of an ICE encounter is real for millions of families across America. Trust Circle gives you a concrete plan: when an encounter begins, one tap activates live video streaming, GPS location sharing, and instant alerts to your trusted circle — including family members, attorneys, and advocacy organizations. Documentation can make a critical difference in outcomes.

Know Your Rights with Circle Chat

Trust Circle's built-in Circle Chat provides instant access to information about your rights during an ICE encounter. Learn what to say, what not to say, whether you need to open your door, and when you can remain silent. Having this information at your fingertips — before an encounter happens — reduces panic and helps you make informed decisions in the moment.

Alert Your Network Instantly

When you activate Trust Circle during an encounter, your trusted circle receives simultaneous alerts via push notification, SMS, and email. They see your live GPS location and can view your video stream in real time. This means your attorney can be monitoring the situation, your family knows where you are, and there's a documented record of the entire encounter.

Protect Your Family's Plan

Trust Circle helps families create safety plans before an encounter happens. Store important documents in the encrypted Document Vault — IDs, legal documents, emergency contacts for children, attorney information. Set up your trusted circle with the people who need to act immediately. When preparation meets Trust Circle's technology, your family is as protected as possible.

Frequently asked

Can I record an ICE encounter?

In public spaces, you generally have the right to record. Trust Circle allows you to stream and record encounters. Always check local laws and use Circle Chat for guidance.

Will ICE know I'm recording?

Trust Circle runs on your phone like any other app. You can choose to hold your phone visibly or have it stream from your pocket. The app doesn't make obvious sounds or alerts.

Can my attorney watch the live stream?

Yes. Add your attorney to your trusted circle and they can view your live stream and location in real time during an encounter.

Does Trust Circle share my data with the government?

No. Trust Circle does not share your data with any government agency. All streams and data are encrypted end-to-end and only accessible to your chosen trusted circle.

How do I set up a family safety plan?

Add all relevant family members, attorneys, and trusted friends to your circle. Store important documents in your vault. Practice activating SOS so everyone knows what to do.

Is Trust Circle available in Spanish?

Trust Circle is working on multi-language support. The Circle Chat feature can provide information in multiple languages.
