Why People Are Switching from Life360

Life360 has millions of users, but growing concerns about data privacy, constant surveillance dynamics in families, and data selling practices have left many looking for alternatives. Trust Circle offers the core safety features people love — location sharing, emergency alerts, and crash detection — built on consent-based sharing, with data encrypted in transit and at rest.

Consent-Based, Not Surveillance-Based

The fundamental difference between Trust Circle and Life360 is philosophy. Life360 defaults to always-on tracking. Trust Circle puts the user in control: you share your location when you choose, with whom you choose, for as long as you choose. This consent-based approach builds trust instead of resentment — especially important in parent-student relationships.

More Than Just a Dot on a Map

Trust Circle goes beyond location sharing with features Life360 doesn't offer: encrypted live video streaming to your circle, multi-channel SOS alerts (push + SMS + email), a secure Document Vault for licenses and IDs, Drive With Confidence for traffic stop protection, and Circle Chat for instant safety guidance. It's a complete personal safety network, not just a tracker.

We Don't Sell Your Data. Period.

Life360 has faced scrutiny for selling precise location data to data brokers. Trust Circle takes a fundamentally different approach: your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with third parties. The core app is free with no ads, and your location data is never sold.

Frequently asked

How is Trust Circle different from Life360?

Trust Circle is consent-based (you control when to share), encrypted in transit and at rest, and includes live video streaming, document vault, and traffic stop protection. We don't sell your data to brokers.

Does Trust Circle track me all the time?

No. You control when location sharing is active. Unlike Life360's always-on approach, Trust Circle shares your location only when you choose to activate it.

Can I switch from Life360 easily?

Yes. Download Trust Circle, invite your circle members, and you're ready. There's no migration needed — just set up your new trusted circle.

Does Trust Circle have crash detection like Life360?

Yes. Trust Circle includes crash detection that alerts your circle if a collision is detected, similar to Life360's feature, and free.

Is Trust Circle free?

Yes. Trust Circle is free to download and use. The core safety features are free, with no ads. An optional Premium subscription adds extras.

Is Trust Circle on Android?

Yes. Trust Circle is on the App Store and on Google Play. The Android app is updated less often than the iPhone app, so a few newer features reach iPhone first. People without the app can still get your alerts by text message.
