Trust Circle

Stream encrypted live video to your trusted circle with one tap — your witnesses are always watching.

Your Eyes in Every Situation

When words aren't enough, video tells the full story. Trust Circle lets you stream live, encrypted video directly to your trusted circle the moment you feel unsafe. Whether you're walking home late, meeting a stranger, or encountering a tense situation, your people can see and hear everything in real time — without you needing to explain.

End-to-End Encrypted Streaming

Privacy is non-negotiable. Trust Circle's live video streams are encrypted end-to-end, meaning only your chosen trusted circle can view the footage. Not us. Not anyone else. The stream is paired with your real-time GPS location, giving your circle the full picture — what's happening and exactly where you are.

One-Tap Activation

In an emergency, every second matters. Trust Circle is designed for speed: one tap activates your SOS, which can include a live video stream, location sharing, and instant alerts to your circle. There's no login screen, no settings menu, no delay. Just immediate protection when you need it most.

Evidence When It Counts

Live video isn't just about real-time safety — it's about creating a record. Trust Circle saves your streams as encrypted recordings that you can access later. If something happens, you have documented proof. This is especially valuable during encounters with authorities, confrontations, or any situation where accountability matters.

Frequently asked

How do I start a live stream?

Activate your SOS in Trust Circle with one tap. You can configure your SOS to automatically include live video streaming along with location sharing and alerts.

Who can see my live stream?

Only the trusted circle members you've chosen. Streams are encrypted end-to-end — Trust Circle employees cannot view them.

Does streaming use a lot of data?

Trust Circle optimizes video quality for reliable streaming even on cellular connections. Typical usage is comparable to a video call.

Can I stream without activating SOS?

Yes. You can share your live video with your circle independently of the SOS feature for situations where you want extra eyes without a full alert.

Are streams saved?

Yes. Streams are recorded and encrypted. You can review, download, or delete them anytime from the app.

Does this work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Trust Circle's live streaming works on both iOS and Android, and your circle can view your stream from either platform.
