All the Safety, None of the Subscription

Noonlight pioneered the personal safety app category, but its paid subscription model puts protection behind a paywall. Trust Circle believes everyone deserves access to safety tools regardless of their budget. That's why every feature — SOS alerts, location sharing, live video streaming, crash detection, and document vault — is completely free.

Your Circle vs. a Call Center

Noonlight routes your alerts through a monitoring center that contacts emergency services on your behalf. Trust Circle takes a different approach: your alerts go directly to your trusted circle — the people who know you, know your situation, and can respond personally. Your circle can call 911, come to your location, or take whatever action is appropriate. Personal connections respond faster and more appropriately than strangers in a call center.

Live Video: A Feature Noonlight Doesn't Have

Trust Circle's live video streaming lets your circle see and hear your situation in real time — not just a location pin. When your roommate can see that someone is following you, or your parent can watch your traffic stop, that's a fundamentally different level of protection than a text alert. Video provides context, evidence, and accountability that location-only alerts can't match.

Built for the Moments That Matter

Trust Circle isn't just an SOS button — it's a comprehensive safety platform. Traffic Stop Protection displays your documents while recording. The Document Vault encrypts your IDs. Circle Chat provides legal guidance. Crash detection alerts your circle automatically. These features work together to protect you in situations Noonlight simply doesn't address.

Frequently asked

Is Trust Circle really free?

Yes, completely free. All features including SOS, live video, location sharing, crash detection, and document vault are available at no cost.

Does Trust Circle contact 911 for me?

Trust Circle alerts your trusted circle and provides one-tap access to 911. Your circle members can also call 911 on your behalf after receiving your alert.

How does the SOS compare to Noonlight's?

Trust Circle's SOS sends multi-channel alerts (push + SMS + email) with GPS location and optional live video to your trusted circle. Noonlight routes alerts through a professional monitoring center.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Trust Circle has no subscription, no premium tier, and no paywalled features. The app is supported by ads.

Can I use Trust Circle on both platforms?

Yes. Trust Circle works on both iOS and Android with identical features. Your circle members can use any device.

What's the catch?

There's no catch. Trust Circle is free and supported by ads. We don't sell your data or location information. Your safety shouldn't depend on your credit card.
