Trust Circle

Explore the world confidently — your trusted circle is always one tap away, no matter where you are.

Adventure Without the Anxiety

Solo travel is one of the most rewarding experiences in life — but it comes with real safety concerns. Navigating unfamiliar cities, meeting strangers, and being far from your support network can feel isolating. Trust Circle bridges that gap by keeping your trusted circle connected to your journey, wherever you go. Share your location, stream live when you feel uneasy, and know that help is always one tap away.

Real-Time Location Sharing Across Borders

Trust Circle works anywhere you have a data connection. Share your real-time location with family back home as you explore a new city, hike a remote trail, or navigate a busy market. Your circle can see exactly where you are on a map, giving both you and them peace of mind. When you arrive at your hotel safely, they'll know — without you needing to send a text.

Circle Chat: Your Travel Safety Expert

Heading to a new country? Circle Chat provides instant access to emergency numbers worldwide, safety tips for your destination, common scams to watch for, and general travel safety guidance. It's like having a knowledgeable travel companion in your pocket — available 24/7, no matter your time zone.

SOS That Works Anywhere

If something goes wrong, Trust Circle's SOS sends instant alerts to your trusted circle with your GPS location and optional live video — whether you're in downtown Tokyo or a rural village in Costa Rica. Multi-channel notifications (push, SMS, email) ensure your circle gets the alert even if they're asleep or in a meeting.

Frequently asked

Does Trust Circle work internationally?

Yes. Trust Circle works anywhere you have an internet connection — cellular data or Wi-Fi. All features including SOS, location sharing, and live video function globally.

Will it work without cell service?

Trust Circle requires an internet connection to send alerts and stream. On Wi-Fi, all features work. GPS tracking works offline and syncs when you reconnect.

Can Circle Chat tell me emergency numbers for my destination?

Yes. Circle Chat provides emergency numbers, safety tips, and general guidance for destinations worldwide.

Can I share my location with people in different time zones?

Absolutely. Your circle sees your real-time location regardless of their time zone. Alerts arrive instantly regardless of time differences.

Should I share my location the entire trip?

That's up to you. Many solo travelers share location during transit and exploration, then pause when settled at their accommodation. You're always in control.

Can I add a local contact I meet while traveling?

Yes. You can add and remove circle members at any time, including trusted people you meet on your travels.
