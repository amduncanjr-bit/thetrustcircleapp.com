Why Traffic Stop Recording Matters

Encounters during traffic stops are some of the most anxiety-inducing moments for drivers. Without documentation, it's your word against theirs. Trust Circle's Drive With Confidence feature changes that by letting you record the entire interaction while digitally displaying your license, insurance, and registration — so you never have to reach for your glove box.

How Drive With Confidence Works

When activated, Trust Circle puts your driver's license, insurance card, and vehicle registration on your phone screen for the officer to view. Simultaneously, the app records video and audio of the interaction and can stream it live to your trusted circle. Your contacts receive real-time alerts with your GPS location, so they know exactly where you are and what's happening.

Hands-Free, Stress-Free

You shouldn't have to fumble through your wallet or glove compartment during a stressful stop. Trust Circle's digital document display keeps your hands visible and on the wheel. Everything is pre-loaded in your secure Document Vault, ready for one-tap activation. The recording runs in the background — encrypted end-to-end — so you have proof of the encounter without escalating tension.

Built for Accountability & Peace of Mind

Trust Circle isn't about confrontation. It's about transparency. Both drivers and officers benefit when encounters are documented. Your recordings are encrypted and stored securely — only accessible to you and the trusted circle members you choose. Whether you're a daily commuter, rideshare driver, or parent teaching your teen to drive, Drive With Confidence adds a critical layer of protection.

Frequently asked

Does Trust Circle record during traffic stops?

Yes. The Drive With Confidence feature records video and audio during traffic stops while simultaneously displaying your documents on screen. Recording is encrypted and only accessible to you and your chosen trusted circle.

Is it legal to record a traffic stop?

In most U.S. states, you have the right to record police encounters in public. Trust Circle's built-in Circle Chat can provide state-specific guidance. Always check your local laws and remain respectful during any encounter.

Can officers see what's on my screen?

Yes — that's the point. Trust Circle displays your license, insurance, and registration clearly so the officer can view them without you reaching into your pockets or glove box.

Does the live stream go to 911?

The live stream goes to your trusted circle members, not to emergency services. However, Trust Circle provides quick-access buttons to call 911 if needed.

What happens to the recording after the stop?

Recordings are encrypted and stored securely. You can review, download, or delete them at any time. Only you and your trusted circle can access the footage.

Do I need a subscription for this feature?

No. Trust Circle is free to download and use. All core safety features, including Drive With Confidence, are available at no cost.
