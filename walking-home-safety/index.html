The Walk Home Shouldn't Be the Scariest Part of Your Day

Walking home alone — from work, the gym, a night out, or a late class — is the single most common safety concern people face. The unease of dark streets, quiet neighborhoods, and unfamiliar routes is something nearly everyone has felt. Trust Circle turns that solo walk into a watched walk, connecting you with your trusted circle in real time so someone always knows where you are.

Share Your Route in Real Time

When you start walking, activate Trust Circle's location sharing. Your trusted circle sees your position on a live map, tracking your route as you move. If you stop unexpectedly, deviate from your usual path, or don't arrive when expected, they'll notice. No calls needed, no texts to type — just quiet, automatic protection.

Stream When Something Feels Off

Gut feelings exist for a reason. If someone is following you, a car is driving slowly beside you, or something just doesn't feel right, tap to stream live video to your circle. They can see and hear your surroundings in real time. Often, just knowing someone is watching is enough to feel safer — and if someone approaches you, the recording creates accountability.

SOS When You Need It

If your walk goes from uncomfortable to dangerous, one tap sends your SOS: your circle gets instant alerts via push, SMS, and email with your exact location and live video. Trust Circle also provides quick access to 911. In the moments when you need to focus on your safety — running, getting to a safe location — your app is doing the alerting for you.

Frequently asked

How do I share my walk with my circle?

Open Trust Circle and activate location sharing. Your circle will see your real-time position on a map. You can also add live video streaming for extra protection.

Does it work in areas with poor signal?

GPS tracking works even with weak signal. Location updates will sync when connectivity improves. For live video, a basic cellular connection is sufficient.

Can my circle see my usual route?

Your circle sees your real-time position. If you regularly walk the same route, any deviation will be noticeable to attentive circle members.

What if I want to stop sharing when I get home?

You can stop location sharing at any time with one tap. Many users configure automatic stop-sharing when they arrive at saved locations like home.

Is this useful for runners and joggers too?

Absolutely. Runners, joggers, and cyclists all benefit from real-time location sharing and SOS access while exercising alone.

Does the live stream alert the other person?

No. Trust Circle streams discreetly from your phone. There's no sound or visual indicator that would alert someone nearby that you're streaming.
